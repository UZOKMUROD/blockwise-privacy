Blockwise Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Blockwise is a block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

Blockwise does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves a small amount of data only on your device:

  • your settings (theme, calm mode, sound and vibration),
  • your best scores and game statistics (for example games played and lines cleared),
  • the game in progress and your daily puzzle progress, so you can continue later.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. You can erase your statistics and best scores at any time in Stats → Reset statistics. Uninstalling Blockwise, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Permissions

Blockwise does not request any Android permissions that give it access to personal data, such as your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or the internet. It uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Third parties

Blockwise contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs. The game is distributed through Google Play, and Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or update apps: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Blockwise is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of Blockwise ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
